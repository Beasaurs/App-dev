**Season 1 (2017)**
> After a money-laundering deal with a Mexican drug cartel goes south, financial planner Marty Byrde moves his family from Chicago to the Ozarks. To keep everyone alive, Marty promises to start a bigger laundering operation in Missouri. Once the Byrdes get there, they get mixed up with local crooks and shady officials.

**Season 2 (2018)**
> Marty and Wendy Byrde are trying to grow their money washing business, but they've got problems piling up from the cartel, the Snells, and the Kansas City mob. Wendy's getting deeper into things and seems to have a taste for power and isn't afraid to get her hands dirty. Things get really tense as the Byrdes start planning a casino to clean even more cash.
